# Selected Delivery Case Studies

These summaries demonstrate Seranna Ramlochan’s approach to delivery while intentionally protecting confidential organisational, technical and personal data. They also show how learning developed through assignments on the Oxford AI-Driven Business Transformation Executive Programme has been applied and progressed in live professional assignments.

## Applied Professional Development

My work brings together practical programme delivery and continuing academic and professional development. Through Oxford programme assignments, I explored how organisations move from AI experimentation to responsible transformation, including leadership, operating-model change, value realisation and the controls needed for adoption at scale.

I have applied and developed this learning through my assignments by aligning AI governance approaches with the principles of the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001. This has included introducing structured ethical governance assessments and AI impact assessments, risk-tiered assurance, defined human oversight, evidence-based approval gates and in-life monitoring expectations.

The resulting frameworks are organisational delivery approaches informed by this learning and wider professional practice; they are not presented as frameworks formally endorsed by the University of Oxford.

## AI Innovation and Responsible Governance

**Context:** A local government organisation was expanding its use of generative AI across productivity, service innovation and data-informed use cases.

**Leadership contribution:** Led the AI programme across multiple delivery squads; integrated delivery roadmaps; established an executive assurance forum; and introduced Ethical Governance Assessment and AI Impact Assessment frameworks. The approach drew on learning progressed through Oxford programme assignments and was aligned with relevant principles from the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001. It connected ethical risk, human oversight, architecture, security, privacy, equality, supplier assurance, training and operational readiness within a proportionate delivery pathway.

**Value:** Created a consistent route from opportunity assessment to controlled testing, deployment and in-life review. This helped leaders make evidence-based decisions, demonstrated how ethical assurance could be embedded into delivery, and maintained clear human accountability for AI-enabled outcomes.

## Enterprise Copilot and AI Agent Adoption

**Context:** Enterprise AI capabilities needed to progress from pilot activity into wider organisational adoption.

**Leadership contribution:** Coordinated platform, governance, adoption and delivery workstreams; defined development pathways for engineering teams and approved power users; and established readiness expectations. Applied responsible transformation learning from the Oxford programme by treating governance, workforce adoption and value realisation as part of the delivery model. Agent delivery was aligned with architecture, cyber, information governance, human oversight and service-management controls.

**Value:** Reframed adoption as an operating-model change rather than a software launch, providing clearer ownership, safer pathways and measurable service outcomes.

## End User Computing Modernisation

**Context:** A large public-sector workforce required modern, secure and supportable end-user technology.

**Leadership contribution:** Led procurement and implementation for 4,000+ staff, including Windows 11, Microsoft Intune and cross-platform controls. Directed deployment readiness, release waves, adoption, stakeholder engagement, supplier coordination and transition into business-as-usual support.

**Value:** Combined technical modernisation with business readiness, creating a repeatable approach to cutover, compliance and user transition.

## Digital Risk and Digital Twin Deployment

**Context:** Digital solutions were being developed for complex, safety-critical offshore and onshore environments.

**Leadership contribution:** Defined the deployment strategy, release roadmap and readiness standards; coordinated international teams across engineering, development, support and change; and managed parallel workstreams and dependencies.

**Value:** Established consistent deployment governance across regions and improved clarity around sequencing, ownership, adoption and handover.

## Cross Government Product Governance

**Context:** Machinery of Government changes created new organisational boundaries and interdependent policy and technology priorities.

**Leadership contribution:** Built delivery capability, established a cross-department Product Council, aligned roadmaps and suppliers, mapped dependencies and escalated delivery risk to senior stakeholders.

**Value:** Helped maintain coherence across programmes supporting net zero, energy affordability, energy security and industrial decarbonisation outcomes.
