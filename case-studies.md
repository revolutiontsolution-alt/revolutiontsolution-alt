# Selected Delivery Case Studies

These summaries demonstrate Seranna Ramlochan’s approach to delivery while intentionally protecting confidential organisational, technical and personal data.

## AI Innovation and Responsible Governance

**Context:** A local government organisation was expanding its use of generative AI across productivity, service innovation and data-informed use cases.

**Leadership contribution:** Led the AI programme across multiple delivery squads; integrated delivery roadmaps; established an executive assurance forum; developed risk-tiered assessment and impact frameworks; strengthened supplier assurance; and connected policy, training, architecture, security, privacy and operational readiness.

**Value:** Created a consistent route from opportunity assessment to controlled testing, deployment and in-life review, helping leaders make evidence-based decisions while preserving human accountability.

## Enterprise Copilot and AI Agent Adoption

**Context:** Enterprise AI capabilities needed to progress from pilot activity into wider organisational adoption.

**Leadership contribution:** Coordinated platform, governance, adoption and delivery workstreams; defined development pathways for engineering teams and approved power users; established readiness expectations; and aligned agent delivery with existing architecture, cyber, information governance and service-management controls.

**Value:** Reframed adoption as an operating-model change rather than a software launch, providing clearer ownership, safer pathways and measurable service outcomes.

## End User Computing Modernisation

**Context:** A large public-sector workforce required modern, secure and supportable end-user technology.

**Leadership contribution:** Led procurement and implementation for 4,000+ staff, including Windows 11, Microsoft Intune and cross-platform controls. Directed deployment readiness, release waves, adoption, stakeholder engagement, supplier coordination and transition into business-as-usual support.

**Value:** Combined technical modernisation with business readiness, creating a repeatable approach to cutover, compliance and user transition.

## Digital Risk and Digital Twin Deployment

**Context:** Digital solutions were being developed for complex, safety-critical offshore and onshore environments.

**Leadership contribution:** Defined the deployment strategy, release roadmap and readiness standards; coordinated international teams across engineering, development, support and change; and managed parallel workstreams and dependencies.

**Value:** Established consistent deployment governance across regions and improved clarity around sequencing, ownership, adoption and handover.

## Cross Government Product Governance

**Context:** Machinery of Government changes created new organisational boundaries and interdependent policy and technology priorities.

**Leadership contribution:** Built delivery capability, established a cross-department Product Council, aligned roadmaps and suppliers, mapped dependencies and escalated delivery risk to senior stakeholders.

**Value:** Helped maintain coherence across programmes supporting net zero, energy affordability, energy security and industrial decarbonisation outcomes.
